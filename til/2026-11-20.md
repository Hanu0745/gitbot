## TIL: Creating an Agent

Today, I learned about the foundational concepts involved in creating an agent. Agents are typically designed to perform specific tasks, make decisions autonomously, or interact with environments. Understanding how to conceptualize and build these entities is crucial for developing systems that require intelligent or automated behavior. While the specifics can vary greatly depending on the context and platform, this session covered the initial principles behind bringing an agent to life.
